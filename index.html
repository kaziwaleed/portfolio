About

I'm a creative who can write the script, run the pitch, sit in the edit and still care about the brief.

I started in production houses, owning budgets, crews and post, and moved into agencies to shape the ideas themselves. That mix is still how I work. I want the insight to be sharp, and I want it to survive the shoot.

Today I lead brand strategy, creative direction and content production at Helium Marketing for PepsiCo, Unilever, Barbican and more. Before that I ran the P&G and Philip Morris portfolios at Leo Burnett, and spent three years at MullenLowe on Unilever, Dalda and KIA.

Along the way I've built integrated brand plans for Fortune 500 FMCG brands that lifted affinity by up to 18%, sales by 11% and category share by 20%. I like being in the room where it's decided where the investment goes.
